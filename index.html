REALMBREAK

A villain teleports onto your real floor. Cup your hands to charge a blast, crack your room open, and shatter it into a sunlit arena for one last beam clash.

Best on Meta Quest 3 / Pro in the Quest Browser (passthrough + hand tracking; controllers work too). Desktop & phone: hold click/tap to charge, click to fire, mash to win. Press Esc to leave. Original characters, art and audio · MIT licensed.
